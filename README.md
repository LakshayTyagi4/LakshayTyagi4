## Now building

I work with real-world, messy datasets — government payroll data, transit records, healthcare queries — and turn them into dashboards and tools people actually use. Currently exploring the intersection of **BI dashboarding**, **cloud data warehousing (Snowflake)**, and **applied GenAI**.

| Signal | What it means |
|---|---|
| `ingest` | Raw, messy, real-world data becomes structured and queryable. |
| `model` | Transform and shape data into something analysis-ready. |
| `decide` | A dashboard or tool that turns numbers into an actual decision. |
